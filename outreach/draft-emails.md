# Draft emails

**Drafts only. Nothing here has been sent.** Written 26 August 2026 to accompany
[`docs/18`](../docs/18-outreach-and-funding.md).

Rules these follow, because they are what gets replies:

- **Short.** Under 150 words. Long emails from strangers do not get read.
- **A question, not a pitch.** The ask is ten minutes of judgement, not a meeting.
- **Easy to say no to.** People answer honestly when refusing is cheap.
- **No jargon.** No "commit-reveal", no "cryptographic", no "distributed", no
  "blockchain", no "Holochain", no "protocol". If a word would need explaining, it loses
  the reader before the question arrives.
- **No claim we cannot back.** The data is synthetic apart from the vessel track, and
  every draft says so.

Replace `[NAME]` and check the current author or contact on the page before sending. Send
**two**, then wait — not ten.

## What not to say, in any of them

- Do not mention Holochain, blockchain, distributed ledgers, or cryptography. The sector
  watched TradeLens fail and the words carry that. [`docs/08`](../docs/08-external-evidence-and-the-real-gap.md)
  and [`docs/04`](../docs/04-strategic-assessment.md) both make this point.
- Do not say the demonstration proves any carrier misreported anything. It does not. The
  parties are invented; only the track is real.
- Do not quote a monetary figure or a confidence score. `docs/08` is explicit that
  adjudication is a liability, not a feature.
- Do not ask for a meeting in the first email. Ask a question.
