# 18 — Who to contact, and when the money opens

**The problem was never "no introductions." It was not knowing which door.** This document
names doors. Draft emails are in [`../outreach/draft-emails.md`](../outreach/draft-emails.md).

Compiled 26 August 2026. Funding status verified against the funders' own pages that day.

## The thing to get straight first

Two different asks have been travelling under one word:

| | What it costs them | What it costs us |
|---|---|---|
| **"Look at this and tell me if it's anything"** | ten minutes | one email |
| **"Run a node / join a protocol / be a pilot partner"** | trust, contracts, IT | months |

Every action below is the first kind. [`docs/16`](16-external-review.md) Part 1 argues the
two got conflated, and that the standing instruction to avoid recruiting people was
correctly applied to the second and wrongly applied to the first.

**Nobody has to be near a port, including us.** Laytime and demurrage is argued by email
between offices.

## Tier 1 — people who have publicly said they care about this

These have written about Notice of Readiness or laytime under their own names. That is a
public declaration of interest, and it makes a cold email warm.

| Who | Why them |
|---|---|
| **Steamship Mutual** — *Notice of Readiness: FAQs* | A P&I club publishing directly on the exact question. Clubs pay the claims, so they carry the cost of unresolved facts |
| **The Shipowners' Club** — *Laytime & Demurrage*, *Notice of Readiness* | Same, and they publish frequently enough to have someone who owns the topic |
| **West of England** — detention defence guide | Their guide is about *when the vessel is in position* — the exact fact AIS settles |
| **Maritime solicitors writing case notes** — e.g. Lester Aldridge on invalid NOR | They write these to be read by people with the problem. Replying is their business model |

**The ask:** send the Phase 2 page, ask whether the fact question shown is one they
actually argue about, and whether the answer would have changed anything.

## Tier 2 — commercial, and financially motivated

**Demurrage consultancies and laytime calculation firms.** A small sector that exists
purely to calculate and recover these claims. They answer email because email is how they
get work, and faster fact-settlement is directly in their interest rather than a threat to
it — they are paid to argue the law, not to establish the position.

## Tier 3 — highest reply rate, lowest stakes

**Maritime law academics.** Swansea, Southampton and Plymouth all have maritime law
institutes. Academics answer cold email at far higher rates than industry, will tell you
plainly if the premise is wrong, and know everyone. If the first three tiers produce
nothing, this is the one that still produces something.

## Tier 4 — only after Phase 0b actually runs

**The firms that argued the cases.** [`docs/17`](17-phase0b-case-candidates.md) identifies
*Tricon Energy Ltd v MTM Trading LLC* [2020] EWHC 700 (Comm) — HFW LLP for Tricon, Lax &
Co LLP for MTM — and *BP Oil International v Target Shipping* [2012] EWHC 1590 (Comm) —
Clyde & Co and Lax & Co.

A letter saying *"I re-derived the arrival time in your matter from public AIS, here is
what it shows"* is not a pitch. It is a specific artefact about their own work.

## Funding calendar

**Every UK call named in the circulated grant list is currently shut.** That was the
correction in [`docs/16`](16-external-review.md) Part 6, and it is why this is a calendar
rather than an action list.

| Programme | Status | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **TRIG** (Connected Places Catapult / DfT) | **Closed.** 2026 round was £2.385m across 53 projects, up to £45k each | Next cohort. Tracks were Maritime Decarbonisation (25), Digital Twins (10), Freight Innovation (5) — **no evidence-integrity track**, so the framing has to bend to one of those |
| **CMDC Round 7** (Innovate UK / DfT) | **Closed 15 July 2026** | Round 8. Priority favours combining vessel or infrastructure themes *with* Smart Shipping, so a pure data project fits poorly on its own |
| **Lloyd's Register Foundation — Maritime Connected** | **Closed** | The best fit of any of them. Remit is safety and evidence; the call was about *"connections where risks are hidden or emerging"* |
| **MarRI-UK** | Rolling round-ups | They publish a maritime funding list; worth a standing check rather than an application |

**Two consequences, both load-bearing.**

**The money is decarbonisation-shaped.** If a grant is the route, the FuelEU / noon-report
use case fits the funding better than laytime does. That is a reason not to discard
Gemini's Gap A even though `docs/08` correctly ranks laytime as the better technical fit.

**Every competition wants a consortium partner** — a trust port, a terminal operator, a
survey firm. So the grants do not route around the recruitment problem. **They converge on
the same email as Tier 1.** The closed window is not a delay; it is the time in which to
find the partner that the next application will require.
