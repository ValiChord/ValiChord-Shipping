OBSERVATIONS (ours, not the source's):

1. All three NAICS codes are in subsector 813 -- Religious, Grantmaking, Civic,
   Professional and Similar Organizations. These are the codes for non-profit
   membership associations. None of them describe software, logistics, or
   freight services. The relevant codes would have been 541511/541512 (custom
   programming / computer systems design) or 488510 (freight transportation
   arrangement). As registered, the company would not surface in federal
   opportunity searches for the work it actually described doing.

2. "patented use of advancements in crypto technology" -- at the time of
   registration the applications were pending, not granted, and both were later
   abandoned. This matches the overstatement the Holochain community identified
   in July 2021; see docs/03.
