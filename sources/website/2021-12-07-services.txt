 Why Holo Sail Tech?

 The global supply chain currently relies on fragmented, expensive, and antiquated solutions...liabilities include cloud-based vulnerabilities, outdated systems, licensing and software fees, blockchain/network bottlenecks, and more. The post-COVID-19 global supply chain faces unprecedented challenges and the world's economic recovery is at risk. 
 Shipping spot rates have gone up 86-400% since early 2020.
 Lack of transparency has led to a global lack of needed equipment.
 There has been a steady rise of Security and Data Breaches worldwide.
 Holo Sail Tech offers a streamlined system with many benefits including enhanced data governance, superior cyber-security, near unlimited scalability, and no licensing fees.
