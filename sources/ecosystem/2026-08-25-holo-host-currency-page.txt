 Currency
 Holo’s approach to pricing, payments and currency .
 Our approach to pricing, payments, and currency reflects our commitment to
 creating a fair and efficient distributed hosting ecosystem.
 Today
 Pricing and payments with HOT and national currencies
 Holo plans to accept payments for hosting services primarily in HoloToken (HOT),
 with additional support for national currencies such as USD and EUR prior to the
 launch of the full Holo platform, and before the implementation of HoloFuel as
 the native hosting backed payment currency in Holo.
 Key detail
 Simplified pricing
 Customer pricing will be aggregated by Holo and simplified for alpha testing
 and provisioning, ensuring straightforward payment processes.
 Key detail
 No micro-payments yet
 Until we implement HoloFuel as the internal accounting system, micro-payments
 and direct peer payments between customers and hosts will not be supported.
 Key detail
 Host price and payment options
 HoloPort owners will be contacted manually as needed if and when hosting
 services to customers or projects arise, based on their hardware’s capacity.
 Hosts will receive compensation for providing alpha hosting services.
 By design
 HoloFuel — the future of hosting payments
 HoloFuel is a mutual-credit cryptocurrency architecture designed for the Holo
 ecosystem. The design intent, as documented in the 2018 Green Paper and
 subsequent whitepapers, encompasses:
 Internal accounting : A system for pricing, payments, and resource allocation within the hosting network
 Host-determined pricing : Hosts set their own rates based on demand and capacity
 Direct peer payments : Supporting customer-to-host payments and micro-transactions
 High-volume capability : Designed for the micro-transaction volume required in a hosting economy
 The implementation status of HoloFuel components is detailed in our
 Product Roadmap . 
 Any HOT redemption or conversion services would be provided by appropriately licensed third-party entities, not Holo Limited. Holo Limited is a cloud hosting company and does not operate token exchange services.
 How it is designed to work
 A currency built for hosting, not for holding
 Unit of account
 HoloFuel will serve as the internal accounting system for pricing, payments,
 and resource allocation.
 Dynamic host-based pricing
 Hosts will set their own rates based on demand and capacity, creating a
 decentralized marketplace for hosting services.
 Direct peer payments
 Customers will pay hosts directly, and micro-payments will be fully supported
 for granular, usage-based settlement.
 Sustainability and scalability
 Designed to handle the high-volume micro-transactions needed in the hosting
 economy.
 History
 Token design history
 Holo Token (HOT) was designed and sold in 2018 as a placeholder token representing a future claim on HoloFuel credits within the ecosystem. The original ICO materials and Green Paper documented that HOT would be redeemable for HoloFuel at a 1:1 ratio once the HoloFuel system became operational. The technical infrastructure and operational arrangements for any such redemption process are subject to ongoing development, regulatory requirements, and will be provided by appropriately licensed entities.
 For the canonical contract addresses, see HOT Token Resources . Any HOT redemption or conversion services would be provided by appropriately licensed third-party entities. Holo Limited is a cloud hosting company and does not operate token exchange services.
 HoloFuel represents our vision of a decentralized, peer-powered hosting network
 where hosts and users interact directly, enabled by fair and efficient payments.
